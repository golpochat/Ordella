---
title: Storefront overview
description: Online ordering for guests.
---

The storefront provides:

- **Menu** browsing by category
- **Basket** and checkout
- **Payment** (when integrated)
- **Order tracking** with live status

Guests access via `{your-subdomain}.{platform-domain}` or your **custom domain**.

Tenant theme (colors, logo) is loaded from the public theme API.
