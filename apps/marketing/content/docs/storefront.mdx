Your storefront is a mobile-first ordering experience on **your** domain—not a generic marketplace listing.

## Highlights

- Commission-free direct orders
- Guest checkout and order tracking
- Theming that follows [Branding](/docs/branding) settings

## Enable the storefront

1. In admin, go to **Channels → Storefront** and turn it on for the location.
2. Review menu visibility (hide items not offered online).
3. Share your storefront URL or connect a custom domain.

## Custom domains

Map `www.yourrestaurant.com` to your tenant:

1. Add the domain in admin under **Domains**.
2. Create the DNS records shown in the setup panel.
3. Wait for verification (usually minutes to a few hours).

<Callout variant="info">
  SSL certificates are provisioned automatically once DNS validates.
</Callout>

## Testing checklist

- [ ] Add to cart, modifiers, and special instructions
- [ ] Checkout with your configured payment method
- [ ] Tracking page updates when the kitchen advances the order
