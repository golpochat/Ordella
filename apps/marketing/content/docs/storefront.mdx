Your storefront is a mobile-first online catalog with checkout and live order tracking—on your brand and domain.

## Features

- Customer checkout and order tracking
- Category navigation and item modifiers
- Promotions when enabled on your plan

## Setup

1. In admin, go to **Channels → Storefront** and turn it on for the location.
2. Review catalog visibility (hide items not offered online).
3. Preview on mobile before sharing your link.

## Custom domain

Map `www.yourbusiness.com` to your tenant:

1. Add the domain in admin under **Settings → Domains**.
2. Create the DNS records shown in admin (CNAME or A, depending on setup).
3. Wait for verification, then enable HTTPS.

## Launch checklist

- [ ] Logo and colors match [Branding](/docs/branding)
- [ ] Tax and delivery fees display correctly at checkout
- [ ] Tracking page updates when fulfillment advances the order
