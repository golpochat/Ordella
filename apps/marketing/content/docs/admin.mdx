The admin dashboard is your command center for products, orders, inventory, staff, and tenant settings.

## Core areas

### Menu management

- Categories, products, modifiers, and availability windows
- Location-specific overrides when you operate multiple sites

### Orders

- Live order queue across POS, storefront, and delivery
- Status overrides, refunds, and reprints (role-dependent)

### Staff & roles

| Role | Typical access |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Billing, branding, all locations |
| Manager | Menu, orders, reports |
| Staff | POS and limited order views |

<Callout variant="warning" title="Permissions">
  Assign the least privilege needed for each team member. Sensitive actions require owner or manager roles.
</Callout>

## Reports

Sales, channel mix, and delivery performance help you decide when to upgrade plans or open new locations.

Continue with [POS](/docs/pos) for in-store setup or [Branding](/docs/branding) to customize your guest experience.
