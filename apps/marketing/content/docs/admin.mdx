The admin dashboard is your command center for catalog, orders, staff, branding, and billing.

## Catalog & inventory management

- Categories, products, modifiers, and variants
- Location-specific overrides when you operate multiple sites
- Stock adjustments and low-stock visibility (where enabled)

### Orders

- Live order board across POS, online, and delivery
- Status overrides and refunds (role-dependent)

## Roles & permissions

| Role | Typical access |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Billing, branding, all locations |
| Manager | Catalog, orders, reports |
| Staff | POS and assigned locations |

## Reports

Sales, channel mix, and delivery performance help you decide when to upgrade plans or open new locations.

Continue with [POS](/docs/pos) for in-store setup or [Branding](/docs/branding) to customize your customer experience.
