---
title: POS setup
description: Configure the point-of-sale app for a location.
---

1. Open the POS app for your location (port 3002 in local dev).
2. Complete the **session** / staff login if prompted.
3. Verify the menu loads from the same catalog as Admin.
4. Run a **test order** and confirm it appears in Admin and KDS.

Staff role requires `pos:access` permission.
