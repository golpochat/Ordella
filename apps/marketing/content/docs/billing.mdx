Ordella billing is tied to **business locations**, **order volume**, and the features you need as you grow.

## Plans at a glance

| Plan | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Single business location pilots |
| Starter | Growing brands (restaurants, cafés, takeaways, grocery, butchers, retail shops, and more) |
| Pro | Multi-location and promotions |
| Enterprise | Custom SLAs and rollouts |

See full pricing on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Usage limits

- **Business locations** — how many sites you can operate
- **Orders per month** — across POS and online channels
- **Features** — e.g. promotions and advanced reports on higher tiers

<Callout variant="info">
  Stripe handles subscriptions and invoices. Owners manage billing in admin under **Settings → Billing**.
</Callout>

## Upgrading

1. Choose a plan on the [pricing page](/pricing) or in admin.
2. Confirm payment method in Stripe Checkout.
3. New limits apply immediately; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
