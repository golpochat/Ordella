Ordella POS is built for retail and food workflows—fast cart building, modifiers, and real-time fulfillment tickets.

## Prerequisites

- An active Ordella tenant
- Publish at least one business location and catalog in [Admin](/docs/admin)

## Enable POS

1. Open **Settings → POS** in admin and enable POS for the location.
2. Pair terminals or open the POS web app on your devices.
3. Run a test order and confirm tickets appear on the fulfillment display.

## Best practices

- Use category tabs and search for high-volume catalogs
- Train staff on modifiers and order notes
- Reconcile cash drawers at shift end

<Callout variant="tip">
  Online and in-store orders share one catalog—price changes in admin apply to both channels.
</Callout>

Pair POS with [Storefront](/docs/storefront) so customers can order online while you serve in-store and pickup orders at the counter.
