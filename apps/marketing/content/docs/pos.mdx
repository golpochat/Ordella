Ordella POS is built for quick-service workflows—fast cart building, modifiers, and real-time kitchen tickets.

## Before you start

- Complete [Tenant Onboarding](/docs/tenant-onboarding)
- Publish at least one location and menu in [Admin](/docs/admin)

## Setup steps

1. Open **Settings → POS** in admin and enable POS for the location.
2. Pair your POS device or browser session (staff login required).
3. Run a **test order** and confirm it appears on [KDS](/docs/getting-started) stations.

## Daily operations

- Use category tabs and search for high-volume menus
- Apply discounts and comps according to your role
- Accept cash or card based on your payment integration

```text
Order flow: POS cart → payment → KDS ticket → completed
```

<Callout variant="tip">
  Online and in-store orders share one menu—price changes in admin apply to both channels.
</Callout>

Pair POS with [Storefront](/docs/storefront) so guests can order online while you serve walk-ins at the counter.
