Delivery connects your [Storefront](/docs/storefront) orders to drivers and customer status updates.

## Enable delivery

1. In admin, open **Channels → Delivery** for the location.
2. Define delivery zones, fees, and minimum order amounts.
3. Invite drivers and install the driver app on their devices.

## Driver workflow

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Assign | Manager or auto-assign sends orders to a driver |
| Pick up | Driver confirms collection from the restaurant |
| Drop off | Proof of delivery and customer notification |

<Callout variant="warning">
  Test zone boundaries with real addresses before promoting delivery to guests.
</Callout>

## Customer experience

Guests see live status in the storefront and customer app—from **preparing** to **on the way** to **delivered**.

Coordinate kitchen timing with [POS](/docs/pos) and KDS so drivers are not waiting on food.
