Delivery connects your online and POS orders to drivers and customer status updates.

## Enable delivery

1. In admin, open **Channels → Delivery** for the location.
2. Define zones, fees, and minimum order values.
3. Invite drivers and install the driver app.

## Driver workflow

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Assign | Admin or auto-assign sends a job to the driver app |
| Pick up | Driver confirms collection from the business |
| Drop off | Proof of delivery and customer notification |

<Callout variant="warning">
  Test zone boundaries with real addresses before promoting delivery to customers.
</Callout>

## Customer experience

Customers see live status in the storefront and customer app—from **preparing** to **on the way** to **delivered**.

Coordinate fulfillment timing with [POS](/docs/pos) and the fulfillment display so drivers are not waiting on orders.
