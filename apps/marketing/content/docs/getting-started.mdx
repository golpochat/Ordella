Ordella is a multi-channel retail platform that unifies **in-store POS**, **online ordering**, **fulfillment displays**, **delivery**, and **customer apps**—with your brand on every screen.

## Why Ordella

- One catalog and one order pipeline across channels
- Multi-tenant architecture for brands and operators
- SaaS billing tied to business locations and order volume

## Architecture at a glance

| Surface | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Storefront | Branded online catalog and checkout |
| POS | In-store, pickup, and counter sales |
| Admin | Catalog, orders, staff, billing, and branding |
| Driver app | Delivery assignments and status |
| Customer app | Reorder, history, and tracking |
| Fulfillment display (FDS) | Real-time fulfillment tickets |
