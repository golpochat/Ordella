Ordella is a multi-tenant restaurant platform that unifies **in-store POS**, **online ordering**, **kitchen displays**, **delivery**, and **customer apps**—with your brand on every screen.

## What you get

- One menu and one order pipeline across channels
- Per-tenant branding, domains, and role-based access
- Admin, staff, driver, and customer experiences out of the box

<Callout variant="tip" title="New here?">
  Create a free tenant first, then follow the Tenant Onboarding guide to go live.
</Callout>

## Platform overview

| Channel | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | Menu, orders, staff, billing, and branding |
| POS | In-store checkout and receipts |
| Storefront | Commission-free online ordering on your domain |
| KDS | Real-time kitchen tickets |
| Delivery | Driver assignments and customer tracking |

## Next steps

1. Read [Tenant Onboarding](/docs/tenant-onboarding) to create your account.
2. Configure [Admin](/docs/admin) and your menu.
3. Enable [POS](/docs/pos) and [Storefront](/docs/storefront) when you are ready to sell.
