---
title: Create your account
description: Sign up and create your first tenant.
---

1. Visit the marketing site and click **Start free trial**.
2. Complete signup with business name, email, and password.
3. You receive a JWT and land in the **onboarding wizard** as tenant admin.

Your tenant starts on the **Free** plan (1 location, 100 orders/month) unless you selected another plan on the pricing page.
