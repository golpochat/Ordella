---
title: Plans and limits
description: Free, Starter, Pro, and Enterprise limits.
---

| Plan | Locations | Orders / month |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Free | 1 | 100 |
| Starter | 3 | 1,000 |
| Pro | Unlimited | 10,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom |

Usage is tracked per billing period. Exceeding limits triggers soft warnings then hard blocks.

Manage plans in **Admin → Settings → Billing** or via `/api/v1/billing/*`.
