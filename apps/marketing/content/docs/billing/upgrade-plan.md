---
title: Upgrade your plan
description: Change subscription via Admin or API.
---

1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Choose **Starter** or **Pro** and attach a payment method (`pm_…` from Stripe).
3. Confirm subscription in Stripe Dashboard (test mode on staging).

**Downgrades** are blocked if current locations or monthly orders exceed the target plan limits.

Enterprise: contact sales@ordella.com.
