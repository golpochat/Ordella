Onboarding connects your business brand to Ordella in a few guided steps—locations, catalog, branding, and channel toggles.

## Create your account

1. Sign up from the marketing site or admin login.
2. Enter your business name and primary contact email.
3. Choose a plan (Free to start).

<Callout variant="info">
  The Free plan includes one business location. Upgrade when you add sites or exceed order limits.
</Callout>

## Onboarding wizard

The wizard walks through:

- **Locations** — address, hours, and timezone
- **Catalog** — categories, products, and modifiers (or import later)
- **Branding** — logo, colors, and theme
- **Channels** — enable POS, storefront, delivery, and apps

## Go-live checklist

- [ ] Catalog published with accurate pricing and tax settings
- [ ] POS tested with a sample in-store or pickup order
- [ ] Storefront tested on mobile (customer checkout + tracking)
- [ ] Fulfillment display receiving tickets from POS and online
- [ ] Delivery zones validated (if using drivers)

See [Billing](/docs/billing) when you are ready to upgrade.
