Onboarding connects your restaurant brand to Ordella in a few guided steps—locations, menu, branding, and channel toggles.

## Create your account

1. Start a **free trial** from the marketing site.
2. Enter your restaurant name and primary contact email.
3. Confirm your tenant subdomain (used for admin and default storefront URLs).

<Callout variant="info">
  The Free plan includes one location. Upgrade when you add sites or exceed order limits.
</Callout>

## Onboarding wizard

The wizard walks through:

- **Locations** — address, hours, and timezone
- **Menu** — categories, products, and modifiers (or import later)
- **Branding** — logo and colors applied across apps
- **Channels** — enable POS, storefront, and delivery when ready

## Go-live checklist

- [ ] Menu published with accurate pricing and tax settings
- [ ] Staff accounts created with appropriate roles
- [ ] Storefront tested on mobile (guest checkout + tracking)
- [ ] POS paired and a test order sent to KDS
- [ ] Delivery rules configured (if applicable)

See [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started) for platform context, then [Billing](/docs/billing) for plan limits.
