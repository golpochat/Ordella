---
title: Branding overview
description: Customize logo, colors, and theme presets.
---

In **Admin → Settings → Branding**:

- Upload **logo** and icon URLs
- Set **primary/secondary** colors and surfaces
- Choose a **preset** (light, dark, custom)
- Preview changes live

Branding applies to Admin, POS, Storefront, and Customer app via `ThemeProvider`.

Public API: `GET /api/v1/public/theme/{tenantId}`
