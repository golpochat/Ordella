---
title: What is Ordella?
description: Overview of the Ordella multi-tenant restaurant platform.
---

Ordella is a **multi-tenant restaurant SaaS platform** that unifies:

- **Admin dashboard** — menu, orders, inventory, staff, settings
- **POS** — in-store checkout
- **Online storefront** — branded direct ordering
- **KDS** — kitchen display with real-time updates
- **Driver app** — delivery workflow
- **Customer app** — order history and tracking

Each **tenant** is an independent restaurant brand with its own subdomain or custom domain, branding, and subscription plan.

## Who is it for?

Independent restaurants, small chains, and growing brands that want one system instead of disconnected POS, marketplace, and delivery tools.
