Branding keeps every Ordella surface feeling like **your** restaurant—not a generic SaaS template.

## Brand kit

In **Settings → Branding**, configure:

- **Logo** — used in admin, storefront header, and emails
- **Primary & accent colors** — buttons, links, and highlights
- **Theme preset** — light, dark, or custom tokens where supported

<Callout variant="tip" title="Consistency">
  Changes propagate to connected apps after save. Refresh open POS or KDS sessions if colors look cached.
</Callout>

## Where branding appears

- Storefront and customer app
- Order confirmation and tracking pages
- Admin login and dashboard accents

## Custom domains

Branding pairs with your domain setup in [Storefront](/docs/storefront)—guests should see your URL and visual identity together.

Review [Billing](/docs/billing) if you need custom themes or white-label options on Enterprise plans.
