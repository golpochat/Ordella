Branding keeps every Ordella surface feeling like **your** business—not a generic SaaS template.

## What you can customize

- Logo (light and dark backgrounds)
- Primary and accent colors
- Theme presets (light, dark, custom)

## Where branding appears

- Admin header and login
- POS and fulfillment display
- Storefront and customer app
- Driver app accents

Branding pairs with your domain setup in [Storefront](/docs/storefront)—customers should see your URL and visual identity together.
