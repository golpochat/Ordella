A **KDS** replaces printed chits with a live queue synced to POS and online orders.

## Benefits

- **Real-time** updates when items are fired or bumped
- **Station views** for grill, salad, or expo
- **Fewer missed** online + in-store tickets

Ordella broadcasts order events over WebSockets on the `/kds` namespace.

See [KDS in features](/features#kds) and [architecture](/docs/getting-started/architecture-overview).
