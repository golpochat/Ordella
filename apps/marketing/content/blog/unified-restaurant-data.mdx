Operators often start with a POS here, an online marketplace there, and delivery in a third app. Each tool has its own menu export, tax settings, and end-of-day report.

## The cost of fragmentation

- **Slow decisions** — leadership reconciles CSVs instead of reading one dashboard
- **Guest friction** — loyalty and reorder break when channels do not share identity
- **Staff churn** — every new tool is another login and another exception path

## What unified data looks like

One tenant in Ordella means:

1. **One menu** powering POS, storefront, and apps
2. **One order pipeline** into kitchen and delivery
3. **One billing relationship** tied to locations and volume

```text
Guest order → API → Admin + KDS + reporting (same event stream)
```

## Practical first step

You do not need a big-bang migration. Most brands enable **direct ordering** first, then align POS and kitchen on the same menu.
