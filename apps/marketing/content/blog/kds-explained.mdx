A **fulfillment display** replaces printed chits with a live queue synced to POS and online orders.

## Benefits

- **Real-time** updates when items are fired or bumped
- **Station views** for prep, packing, or expo
- **Fewer missed** online + in-store tickets

Ordella broadcasts order events over WebSockets so stations stay in sync without refreshing.

## When to add a fulfillment display

<Callout variant="warning">
  If online volume is growing, paper tickets often fail before front-of-house does. A fulfillment display is usually the first ops upgrade after direct ordering.
</Callout>
