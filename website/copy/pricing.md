## Pricing philosophy

### Section headline options

1. **One platform. Predictable growth.**
2. **How we think about pricing**
3. **Value aligned with scale—not shelfware**

### Subheadline options

1. **Base platform + transparent usage—not hidden integration fees.**
2. **Enterprise controls without punishing mid-market operators.**
3. **Advanced modules priced where they deliver ROI.**

### Copy (paragraphs)

Ordella’s **pricing philosophy** starts from the Retail OS model: customers should not pay twice for the same truth across POS, ecommerce, and analytics. Platform tiers unlock core operations and commerce modules; **usage-based** components reflect API calls, webhook deliveries, storage, and optional intelligence or autonomy workloads that scale with the business.

We separate **access** from **consumption** so a ten-location grocer and a thousand-location enterprise can both find a fair entry point—enterprises add custom regions, SLAs, and compliance packaging without forcing mid-market buyers onto an “enterprise only” product.

Partners and marketplace economics are documented in the partner program; retailer pricing stays independent of partner revenue share.

Honest labeling matters: preview modules may be included in early-access tiers with clear boundaries until GA.

### Section headline options

1. **Pay for what you use at scale**
2. **Usage-based components**
3. **APIs, events, and intelligence meters**

### Subheadline options

1. **Meters for API requests, webhook deliveries, storage, and compute-heavy modules.**
2. **Transparent dashboards in the Developer Portal.**
3. **Overage rules published before you enable production.**

### Copy (paragraphs)

**Usage-based pricing** (placeholder meters) aligns cost with scale:

- **API requests** — counted per tenant per billing period; soft limits with upgrade prompts.  
- **Webhook deliveries** — successful deliveries and retries per policy in [Webhooks](../../docs/public/developers/webhooks.md).  
- **Data lake storage & export** — metered storage and egress for analytics workloads.  
- **Intelligence & autonomy** — optional meters for model inference, vision pipelines, or simulation compute when enabled.

Usage appears in the Developer Portal billing views (see [billing-overview](../../developer-portal/sections/billing-overview.md)). Alerts notify admins before hard limits where configured.

Exact unit prices and included bundles per tier are **not final** on this page—request a current rate card from sales.
