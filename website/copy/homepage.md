## Problem statement

### Section headline options

1. **Retail fractured. Margins followed.**
2. **Too many tools. Not enough truth.**
3. **The stack grew. Clarity did not.**

### Section subheadline options

1. **Disconnected systems slow every shift—and every channel your customers use.**
2. **When inventory, pricing, and customer data live in silos, operators pay in margin and momentum.**
3. **Modern shoppers expect one experience. Legacy patchwork cannot deliver it.**

### Copy (paragraphs)

Retail did not fall behind overnight. It fractured. One vendor runs the point of sale. Another runs the website. Inventory lives in a spreadsheet—or a warehouse system that never talks to the store floor. Marketing fires campaigns into the void because customer data sits in five places that do not agree on who the shopper is.

Merchants reconcile reports by hand. They discover stockouts after customers leave empty-handed. They launch promotions that conflict with store pricing because nothing shares a single source of truth. Growth means adding tools, not gaining clarity—and every new integration is another fee, another login, another failure point.

Staff feel it every shift. Associates juggle disconnected apps to look up inventory, apply discounts, or answer a simple question about an order. Training multiplies with every system. Customers have no patience for the gap: they expect instant answers, consistent prices, and seamless pickup whether they shop on a phone or in aisle three.

The crisis is not a lack of software. It is **fragmentation**—too many partial solutions for a business that must run as one.

### Section headline options

1. **One operating system for the whole business**
2. **Unify operations, commerce, and intelligence**
3. **Replace the patchwork with a Retail OS**

### Section subheadline options

1. **Ordella delivers one tenant model, one event stream, and modular systems that share data by design.**
2. **When something changes in one channel, every system that needs to know—knows.**
3. **Built real-time, AI-native, and autonomous where it counts—global from day one.**

### Copy (paragraphs)

Ordella is **the Retail Operating System**: a single platform that unifies store operations, commerce, data, and intelligence for retailers of every size. Catalog, orders, inventory, pricing, loyalty, and fulfillment connect through shared domain services—not brittle point-to-point integrations.

When a sale happens anywhere, the platform records it on the Event Bus so analytics, orchestration, and partner apps can react without polling overnight exports. Operators see current state across locations. Developers build on versioned APIs. Enterprise teams govern tenants, regions, and compliance from one cloud foundation.

You do not need another app in the stack. You need a system designed for how retail actually runs today—and how it will run tomorrow.

## Digital twins explanation

### Section headline options

1. **See the store before you change it**
2. **Digital twins for planning—not guesswork**
3. **Model reality. Test decisions safely.**

### Section subheadline options

1. **Virtual models of stores, assets, and live state—synchronized from operations and IoT.**
2. **Run what-if scenarios without disrupting the shop floor.**
3. **Align planograms, staffing, and energy use with what is actually happening.**

### Copy (paragraphs)

**Digital Twins** on Ordella are live software models of your stores: layout, devices, inventory positions, and environmental signals mirrored from operations data and edge deployments. Twins stay aligned with reality so planners and operators work from current state—not stale floor plans.

Use twins to simulate planogram changes, staffing levels, or fulfillment flows in a **forecasting sandbox** before rolling out to live locations. Pair simulations with the Autonomous Engine and IoT integrations when you need closed-loop sensing and action.

Digital twins turn “what if we…” into a governed workflow—with documentation for architects and operators alike.

## Integrations overview

### Section headline options

1. **Connect every channel on one API**
2. **POS, storefront, mobile, IoT, partners—one surface**
3. **Integrate once. Stay in sync.**

### Section subheadline options

1. **REST APIs, webhooks, and integration guides for the retail stack you already run.**
2. **Your apps speak to Ordella; Ordella broadcasts truth on the Event Bus.**
3. **Built API-first so developers and partners innovate without custom glue code.**

### Copy (paragraphs)

Ordella is **API-first** by design. Point-of-sale, ecommerce storefronts, mobile apps, IoT devices, and partner solutions all call the same versioned REST API with tenant context. When events occur—orders placed, stock adjusted, promotions applied—**webhooks** deliver signed callbacks so your automations react in real time.

Integration guides cover [POS](../../docs/public/guides/pos-integration.md), [storefront](../../docs/public/guides/storefront-integration.md), [mobile](../../docs/public/guides/mobile-app-integration.md), [IoT](../../docs/public/guides/iot-device-integration.md), and [partner](../../docs/public/guides/partner-integration.md) patterns. SDK documentation and rate limits are published for production planning.

You keep the channels your customers love. Ordella becomes the operating system underneath—one integration tax instead of five.
