## Partner program overview

### Section headline options

1. **The Ordella partner ecosystem**
2. **A program built for serious ISVs**
3. **Co-build, certify, co-sell**

### Subheadline options

1. **Technology partners, solution partners, and strategic alliances—one application process.**
2. **Benefits scale with certification depth and customer success.**
3. **Commercial terms documented before you contract.**

### Copy (paragraphs)

Ordella’s partner program connects builders to retailers running on a **unified tenant and event model**. Partners receive onboarding playbooks, technical review, go-to-market support, and access to partner APIs and sandbox tenants. Program structure—including types, benefits, and requirements—is published in [Partner Program](../../docs/public/partners/partner-program.md).

We prioritize partners who strengthen the platform: deeper integrations, better security posture, and measurable customer outcomes—not logo slides. Applications are reviewed for fit, technical maturity, and support capacity.

Retailers win when partners build once and deploy across tenants with consistent governance.

## Partner benefits

### Section headline options

1. **Why partners build on Ordella**
2. **Benefits that compound with certification**
3. **Technical, commercial, and go-to-market**

### Subheadline options

1. **APIs, events, and tenants—not per-retailer science projects.**
2. **Enablement, certification badges, and partner portal access.**
3. **Co-sell motions for certified solutions.**

### Copy (paragraphs)

**Technical benefits** include sandbox tenants, partner API scopes, webhook testing tools, and architecture office hours for certified partners. **Commercial benefits** include revenue share on qualifying marketplace and attach transactions (see Revenue Share). **Go-to-market benefits** include listing on the marketplace, joint case studies, event presence, and sales enablement kits when you meet certification bars.

Partners inherit Ordella’s **enterprise narrative**: security architecture, compliance overviews, and data residency documentation help you sell to retailers with procurement gates.

Benefits evolve; partners receive notice through program changelog and account teams.

## Partner revenue share

### Section headline options

1. **Transparent commercial mechanics**
2. **Revenue share for qualifying partner transactions**
3. **Build, list, earn—terms in your agreement**

### Subheadline options

1. **Commission tiers and payout schedules documented for approved partners.**
2. **Reporting and export APIs through the partner portal.**
3. **Specific rates provided during contracting—not on this marketing page.**

### Copy (paragraphs)

**Revenue share** applies to qualifying marketplace transactions and subscription attach defined in your partner agreement. Partners access statements and export APIs through the partner portal; tax and invoicing requirements vary by region.

Public documentation in [Revenue Share](../../docs/public/partners/revenue-share.md) summarizes program structure: commission tiers, reporting cadence, payout schedule, and dispute processes. **Numeric rates are not published on the marketing site**—they are shared during contracting so your finance team can model accurately.

Ordella aligns incentives with customer success: partners who deploy well and support tenants well earn renewals—and share in growth.

**Related docs:** [Revenue Share](../../docs/public/partners/revenue-share.md)

## Partner onboarding

### Section headline options

1. **From application to first production tenant**
2. **Onboarding that respects your roadmap**
3. **Certify, list, scale**

### Subheadline options

1. **Technical review, security questionnaire, and launch checklist.**
2. **Documented steps in Partner Onboarding—not a black box.**
3. **Typical path: sandbox → certification → marketplace → co-sell.**

### Copy (paragraphs)

**Partner onboarding** walks you from application through sandbox integration, security review, certification testing, and marketplace launch. [Partner Onboarding](../../docs/public/partners/partner-onboarding.md) lists milestones: legal agreements, technical architecture review, webhook and OAuth validation, support runbooks, and listing assets.

Early partners work with a partner manager; mature partners use self-serve portal workflows where available. Expect candid feedback during certification—we protect retailers’ tenants and brands.

Once live, stay current with API changelog, breaking change notices, and program tier requirements for renewal.
