# Ordella Partner Program — Tiers

Tier structure for the Ordella Partner Program: **Registered**, **Silver**, **Gold**, and **Platinum**. Benefits scale with certification depth, customer success, and go-to-market investment—not tenure alone.

**Related:** [Program overview](./PROGRAM_OVERVIEW.md) · [Certification](./CERTIFICATION.md) · [Support and SLAs](./SUPPORT_AND_SLAS.md) · [Public partner program](../docs/public/partners/partner-program.md)

> **Naming note:** Public docs and website copy may reference *Certified / Premier / Strategic*. Map as: **Silver ≈ Certified**, **Gold ≈ Premier**, **Platinum ≈ Strategic** for customer-facing materials until naming is unified.

---

## Tier structure (summary)

| Tier | Positioning | Marketplace | Revenue share | Lead program |
|------|-------------|-------------|---------------|--------------|
| **Registered** | Building toward certification | No public listing | Not eligible | No |
| **Silver** | Certified app or integration | Eligible | Standard rate (contract) | Pool access |
| **Gold** | Scale + co-sell | Featured placement | Enhanced rate | Priority pool |
| **Platinum** | Strategic alliance | Strategic spotlight | Custom / volume | Dedicated + exclusivity options |

Specific percentages and SLAs are in the partner agreement—not published in marketing ([REVENUE_SHARE.md](./REVENUE_SHARE.md)).

## Registered

### Requirements

- Signed partner agreement (baseline) and NDA where required  
- Completed partner application with company profile and integration intent  
- At least one sandbox tenant and one API key in [Developer Portal](../developer-portal/pages/api-keys.md)  
- Security contact designated; agree to vulnerability disclosure process  

### Benefits

- Partner portal access (limited): onboarding, docs, community  
- Sandbox environment per [sandbox-overview](../developer-portal/sections/sandbox-overview.md)  
- Public documentation and beta/office hours where open  
- Listed in internal partner CRM—not public directory  

### Upgrade path

Advance to **Silver** by completing **Silver certification** ([CERTIFICATION.md](./CERTIFICATION.md#silver-certification)) and marketplace technical review if listing an app.

---

## Silver

### Requirements

- **Silver certification** (developer, integrator, or consultant path)  
- OAuth app or integration passing security questionnaire  
- Webhook reliability: ≥ 95% delivery success over 30-day review window  
- Support: documented runbook + 2 business day email response commitment to joint customers  
- Marketplace listing assets: description, logo, support URL, privacy policy  

### Benefits

- **Marketplace listing** (standard placement)  
- **Revenue share** on qualifying transactions—baseline tier in agreement  
- Partner directory listing on **ordella.com/partners** (placeholder)  
- Co-marketing asset kit ([CO_MARKETING.md](./CO_MARKETING.md))  
- **Support:** Silver SLAs ([SUPPORT_AND_SLAS.md](./SUPPORT_AND_SLAS.md))  
- Access to **lead pool** ([LEAD_DISTRIBUTION.md](./LEAD_DISTRIBUTION.md))  

### Upgrade path

To **Gold**: 12 months at Silver (or 6 with executive approval), ≥ 3 successful retailer deployments (reference checks), customer satisfaction threshold, and Gold certification modules complete.

---

## Gold

### Requirements

- **Gold certification** renewal current  
- ≥ 3 live production tenant deployments or equivalent GMV through marketplace (placeholder thresholds)  
- Dedicated partner manager engagement quarterly  
- Joint business plan submitted annually  
- Participation in at least one co-marketing activity per half ([CO_MARKETING.md](./CO_MARKETING.md))  
- Maintained webhook/API health metrics (same or stricter than Silver)  

### Benefits

- **Featured marketplace** placement and launch PR consideration  
- **Enhanced revenue share** tier in agreement  
- **Priority lead distribution** and deal registration protection windows ([LEAD_DISTRIBUTION.md](./LEAD_DISTRIBUTION.md))  
- **Gold SLAs** including priority ticket queue  
- Early access program for select preview modules (NDA)  
- Joint webinar and event booth opportunities  

### Upgrade path

To **Platinum**: executive nomination, strategic fit review, revenue or ecosystem impact criteria, signed strategic addendum.

---

## Platinum

### Requirements

- Executive sponsorship on both sides  
- Strategic alliance agreement (custom)  
- Minimum revenue or pipeline commitment (placeholder—contractual)  
- Global or regional GTM plan with Ordella field teams  
- Platinum certification for key technical leads (optional path merge)  

### Benefits

- **Custom revenue share** and MDF (market development funds) where applicable  
- **Strategic marketplace** placement and launch co-announcements  
- **Dedicated lead routing** and optional **exclusivity** in segment/geo ([LEAD_DISTRIBUTION.md](./LEAD_DISTRIBUTION.md#exclusivity-rules-if-any))  
- **Platinum SLAs** including named TAM (technical account manager) placeholder  
- Product council participation (roadmap input under NDA)  
- Custom integration and co-engineering slots (capacity-limited)  

---

## Upgrade rules (general)

1. **Certification first** — tier cannot exceed highest active certification level.  
2. **No skip** — Registered → Silver requires Silver cert; Platinum requires Gold history unless executive exception.  
3. **Annual review** — tier benefits revalidated; downgrade possible if metrics or certification lapse ([CERTIFICATION.md — Renewal](./CERTIFICATION.md#renewal-rules)).  
4. **Appeals** — 30-day window to remediate before downgrade; partner manager documents plan.  
5. **Beta partners** — may enter at Silver after beta certification fast-track ([beta ONBOARDING](../beta-program/ONBOARDING.md#partners)).  

Changes communicated via partner portal and email; effective date in agreement amendment or program changelog.
