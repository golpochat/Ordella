## Co-marketing opportunities

Ordella co-marketing helps partners **reach retailers** already evaluating unified retail platforms. Eligible partners (typically **Silver+**) access:

- **Launch announcements** — joint blog/press when marketplace app goes live (editorial review)  
- **Solution briefs** — PDF one-pagers with partner + Ordella positioning ([Messaging pillars](../brand/MESSAGING_PILLARS.md))  
- **Email campaigns** — inclusion in Ordella nurture to opted-in retail audience (segmented, not spam)  
- **Marketplace featuring** — seasonal campaigns highlighting certified apps  
- **MDF (Gold/Platinum)** — market development funds for events/ads per agreement ([REVENUE_SHARE.md](./REVENUE_SHARE.md) separate from commission)

**Registered** partners receive logo usage guidelines and self-serve badges only—no joint campaigns until Silver.

Requests submitted via partner portal **Co-marketing** form ([PARTNER_PORTAL.md](./PARTNER_PORTAL.md))—lead time **4–6 weeks** for major launches.

## Case studies

Case studies prove **measurable outcomes**—not vanity quotes.

### Criteria

- Named retailer with written approval (or anonymized vertical if required)  
- Deployed on Ordella ≥ 90 days (or successful pilot completion)  
- Quantified result: e.g., reduced stockouts, faster integration, hours saved—honest ranges, not unverified ROI %  
- Partner **Silver+** and certification current  

### Process

1. Partner submits draft via portal (800–1200 words + metrics).  
2. Ordella marketing + customer success edit for accuracy and [voice](../brand/VOICE_AND_TONE.md).  
3. Retailer sign-off on final PDF/web version.  
4. Publish on **ordella.com** and partner site with reciprocal links.

Gold+ partners prioritized for launch PR and [press kit](../press-kit/PRODUCT_OVERVIEW.md) references when approved.

## Joint webinars

Quarterly **partner webinar series** on docs.ordella.com / live stream (placeholder):

- Ordella presents platform theme (15 min)  
- Partner presents solution on Ordella (25 min)  
- Q&A moderated (15 min)  

**Silver:** 1 slot per year in rotation pool. **Gold:** 2 slots + optional exclusive vertical webinar. **Platinum:** custom executive webinar with field team.

Partners supply slide template compliance (logo clear space, no competitor disparagement). Recordings posted with consent.
