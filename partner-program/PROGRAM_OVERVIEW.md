# Ordella Partner Program — Overview

The **Ordella Partner Program** is the commercial and technical framework for technology companies, integrators, and consultants who build on **the Retail Operating System**. Partners extend Ordella through marketplace apps, certified integrations, and co-sell motions—reaching retailers on a unified tenant model, API, and event stream instead of rebuilding glue code per customer.

**Related:** [Public partner program](../docs/public/partners/partner-program.md) · [Website partners copy](../website/copy/partners.md) · [Brand messaging](../brand/MESSAGING_PILLARS.md) · [Developer Portal — partner tools](../developer-portal/sections/partner-tools.md)

---

## What the partner program is

Ordella’s partner program is not a referral badge—it is an **ecosystem contract**: technical certification, documented commercial terms (including revenue share), go-to-market support, and access to partner-only portal capabilities. Partners integrate against [Partner API](../docs/public/partners/partner-api.md) and standard platform APIs, validate security and webhook reliability, and optionally publish listings that retailers can install per tenant.

The program aligns with Ordella’s **API-first** platform strategy. If a capability ships for retailers, partners can build on documented contracts with the same tenancy, authentication, and event semantics described in [API overview](../docs/public/developers/api-overview.md) and [Event Bus](../docs/public/systems/event-bus.md). Partner success is measured in **customer outcomes** and **platform health**—see [SUCCESS_METRICS in beta program](../beta-program/SUCCESS_METRICS.md) for parallel beta KPIs.

Internal program docs in `/partner-program/` complement public pages on **docs.ordella.com**; specific rates and SLAs appear in signed partner agreements.

## Who it is for

| Partner type | Description | Typical deliverable |
|--------------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Technology / ISV** | Software vendors with a product (POS extension, analytics, loyalty, hardware) | Marketplace app, OAuth install, webhooks |
| **Systems integrator (SI)** | Agencies implementing Ordella for retailers | Certified integrations, multi-tenant deployments |
| **Consultant** | Retail advisors configuring modules and change management | Certification + services (no marketplace required) |
| **Strategic alliance** | Platforms, clouds, or networks with co-sell at scale | Custom GTM—[PARTNER_TIERS.md](./PARTNER_TIERS.md) Platinum |

Partners must meet [eligibility](../beta-program/ELIGIBILITY.md#partner-eligibility-criteria) standards: registered business, technical owner, security review, and support capacity. Competitors to the core Retail OS may be declined case-by-case.

## Why partners should join

**Technical:** Build once against stable REST and webhook contracts; sandbox tenants and [Developer Portal](../developer-portal/README.md) tools reduce time-to-first-integration. Partner scopes and app lifecycle are documented in [Partner integration guide](../docs/public/guides/partner-integration.md).

**Commercial:** Revenue share on qualifying marketplace and attach transactions ([REVENUE_SHARE.md](./REVENUE_SHARE.md)); tiered commission structure as certification deepens.

**Go-to-market:** Marketplace discovery, partner directory, co-marketing ([CO_MARKETING.md](./CO_MARKETING.md)), and lead distribution for qualified tiers ([LEAD_DISTRIBUTION.md](./LEAD_DISTRIBUTION.md)).

**Enterprise sales:** Inherit Ordella’s security and compliance narrative ([Security architecture](../docs/public/architecture/security-architecture.md), [SOC 2 overview](../docs/public/compliance/soc2-overview.md)) to shorten retailer procurement.

Partners stop renting fragmentation; retailers get solutions that already speak their **tenant truth**.

## Ecosystem vision

Ordella envisions a **Global Partner Network** where certified apps and integrators compound platform value: every installation strengthens the Event Bus graph, every partner app respects tenancy and consent, and every co-sell motion reinforces “one Retail OS” versus another point tool.

Long term, the marketplace becomes the **default distribution layer** for retail innovation on Ordella—ISVs reach tenants with governance, retailers adopt vetted software faster, and Ordella scales without owning every feature. Consultants and SIs ensure complex retailers **land and expand** with operational excellence.

That vision depends on **trust**: certification ([CERTIFICATION.md](./CERTIFICATION.md)), support SLAs ([SUPPORT_AND_SLAS.md](./SUPPORT_AND_SLAS.md)), transparent payouts, and honest roadmap communication ([beta ROADMAP_PREVIEW](../beta-program/ROADMAP_PREVIEW.md) patterns apply to partners).
