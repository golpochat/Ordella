# Ordella Partner Program — Support & SLAs

Support levels, response times, escalation, and partner-only channels by tier. SLAs apply to **partner-to-Ordella** support—not replacement of partner-to-customer obligations.

**Related:** [Partner tiers](./PARTNER_TIERS.md) · [Certification](./CERTIFICATION.md) · [Partner portal](./PARTNER_PORTAL.md) · [Brand tone](../brand/VOICE_AND_TONE.md)

## Support philosophy

Ordella partner support helps partners **build, certify, and sell** successfully. Retailer operational incidents for mutual customers may involve **three-way** coordination—partner front line, Ordella platform back line—especially for P0 tenancy or API outages.

Partners must maintain their own **customer support** commitments; Ordella does not bypass partners except for platform-wide incidents or contractual direct-support deals (Platinum).

## Escalation rules

### Level 1 — Partner support queue

`partners-support@ordella.com` <!-- PLACEHOLDER --> or portal ticket. Include: org ID, tenant ID, request IDs, severity, business impact.

### Level 2 — Partner manager (Silver+)

If no Level 1 response within SLA, partner manager escalates internally. Gold/Platinum have named contacts.

### Level 3 — Engineering duty

P0/P1 platform defects → on-call engineering. Partner receives incident ID and status page link (`<!-- status.ordella.com placeholder -->`).

### Level 4 — Executive

Platinum strategic accounts or recurring P0 → VP Engineering / CPO bridge within 24h of request.

**Joint customer escalations:** Partner remains primary interface to retailer unless incident is purely Ordella infrastructure.

## Partner obligations (mutual SLA)

Partners agree to:

- Maintain certification and security patch cadence for listed apps  
- Respond to mutual customers per published partner support policy (min 2 business days for Silver)  
- Participate in post-incident review when partner code contributed to failure  
- Not publish Ordella customer names without consent  

SLA credits (if any) defined in partner agreement—typically fee credits for missed **Ordella** response targets, not revenue share adjustments.
