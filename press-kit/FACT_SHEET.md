## Key facts

- Ordella is a **multi-tenant Retail Operating System**—one tenant model, one API, one event stream across locations and channels.  
- Platform principles: **real-time** (Event Bus), **AI-native** (AI Assistant + Retail Genome), **autonomous** (Autonomous Retail Engine + Orchestration), **global** (Cloud Platform, regional deployment, data residency options).  
- **API-first** design: versioned REST at `https://api.ordella.com/v1`; webhooks mirror Event Bus types for integrations.  
- **Edge-aware** patterns support store continuity and local processing where latency or connectivity demands it.  
- **Partner ecosystem** with documented program, onboarding, and revenue-share structure for marketplace participants.  
- Public **architecture and compliance** documentation supports enterprise evaluation (SOC 2, GDPR, PCI scope, ISO 27001 overviews).

## Platform capabilities (summary)

**Operations & commerce:** Catalog, inventory, orders, pricing, promotions, loyalty, subscriptions, marketing, support workflows (tenant-scoped).

**Enterprise:** Multi-tenant isolation, regional Cloud Platform, security and deployment architecture docs, compliance program overviews.

**Integrations:** POS, storefront, mobile, IoT, partner apps—documented under `docs/public/guides/`.

## Competitive positioning (press-safe)

Ordella competes at the **platform layer**—how retail runs—not on a single feature checkbox. Differentiators vs. fragmented stacks:

- One OS vs. many vendors to reconcile after close  
- Real-time events vs. batch exports  
- Governed autonomy vs. manual heroics  
- Genome-grounded AI vs. generic dashboards  
- Documented global/compliance story vs. retrofitted enterprise controls
