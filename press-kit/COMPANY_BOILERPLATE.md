## Short description (1 paragraph — ~75 words)

**For immediate use in articles and show notes.**

Ordella is **the Retail Operating System**—a multi-tenant platform that unifies store operations, commerce, data, and intelligence for retailers of every size. Built API-first and event-driven, Ordella connects POS, storefront, admin, mobile, IoT, and partner applications through one tenant model, one API, and one real-time event stream. The platform embeds AI assistance, policy-bound automation, digital twins, and the Retail Genome intelligence graph so teams act on current truth—not yesterday’s exports.

## Standard description (2 paragraphs — ~150 words)

**For press releases and company fact blocks.**

Ordella is **the Retail Operating System**—a unified platform for modern retail. Instead of reconciling disconnected point solutions for inventory, pricing, promotions, ecommerce, and analytics, retailers run on shared domain services: one catalog, one order model, one permissions layer, and one event stream that keeps every channel aligned. When a sale completes in-store or online, Ordella propagates that truth to operations, intelligence, automation, and integrations in real time.

Ordella is built **real-time**, **AI-native**, and **autonomous where it counts**, with **global** multi-tenant cloud and edge patterns for regional deployment and compliance. The AI Assistant and Retail Genome ground copilots and segmentation in operational data; the Autonomous Retail Engine and Orchestration execute policy-bound workflows; Digital Twins enable simulation before changes hit the shop floor. Developers and partners extend the platform through versioned REST APIs, webhooks, and a documented partner program—without re-platforming every year.
