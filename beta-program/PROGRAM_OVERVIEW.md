# Ordella Beta Program — Overview

The Ordella Beta Program is a structured early-access program for **developers**, **technology partners**, and **retailers** who help validate the Retail Operating System before general availability. Participants integrate against real platform contracts—sandbox tenants, versioned APIs, webhooks, and documented modules—while Ordella collects feedback that shapes stability, documentation, and launch readiness.

**Related:** [Brand voice](../brand/VOICE_AND_TONE.md) · [Launch narrative](../launch/LAUNCH_NARRATIVE.md) · [Developer Portal](../developer-portal/README.md) · [Public docs](../docs/public/index.md)

---

## Purpose of the beta

Retail technology fails most often at the **integration layer**: fragmented data, unclear tenancy, and automation that cannot be governed. The beta exists to prove Ordella’s core promise—**one tenant, one API, one event stream**—with real builders and operators before broad launch.

Beta goals include validating API ergonomics and webhook reliability, hardening sandbox-to-production promotion paths, stress-testing partner onboarding and app certification, and confirming that retailers can run pilot locations on unified operations and commerce modules. We label preview capabilities honestly per [changelog](../docs/public/changelog.md) and [brand honesty rules](../brand/VOICE_AND_TONE.md); beta is not a marketing preview with hidden limitations.

Success means participants ship meaningful integrations or pilot stores, provide actionable feedback, and would recommend the platform to peers—measured in [SUCCESS_METRICS.md](./SUCCESS_METRICS.md).

## Who it is for

### Developers

Independent engineers, agency teams, and retailer IT groups building **POS, storefront, mobile, IoT, or internal tools** on Ordella APIs. Ideal participants have production integration experience and can complete a first API + webhook milestone within the beta window.

*See [ELIGIBILITY.md — Developers](./ELIGIBILITY.md#developer-eligibility-criteria)* · [ONBOARDING_FLOW.md — Developers](./ONBOARDING_FLOW.md#developers)*

### Partners

ISVs and technology companies pursuing **marketplace listing**, OAuth apps, or co-sell motions. Partners should align with [Partner program](../docs/public/partners/partner-program.md) requirements and commit to certification steps during beta.

*See [ELIGIBILITY.md — Partners](./ELIGIBILITY.md#partner-eligibility-criteria)* · [Partner onboarding docs](../docs/public/partners/partner-onboarding.md)*

### Retailers

Operators running **pilot locations or brands** on Ordella for operations, commerce, and intelligence modules. Retailers may join directly or through a certified integration partner; enterprise prospects with formal security review needs are routed to a dedicated cohort.

*See [ELIGIBILITY.md — Retailers](./ELIGIBILITY.md#retailer-eligibility-criteria)*

## What participants get

| Audience | Benefits (beta) |
|----------|-----------------|
| **All** | Sandbox tenant, documentation access, beta support channel, roadmap preview under NDA where applicable |
| **Developers** | Elevated sandbox rate limits (placeholder), early API reference updates, office hours |
| **Partners** | Partner sandbox, certification fast-track queue, co-marketing opportunities for launch references |
| **Retailers** | Pilot tenant configuration, implementation office hours, pricing preview (non-binding) |

Participants receive access to the **Developer Portal** ([dashboard](../developer-portal/pages/dashboard.md), [API keys](../developer-portal/pages/api-keys.md), [webhooks](../developer-portal/pages/webhooks.md), [sandbox](../developer-portal/pages/sandbox.md)) and public **docs.ordella.com** as the source of truth for contracts.

Beta does **not** guarantee SLA, unlimited production usage, or feature permanence—see [ROADMAP_PREVIEW.md](./ROADMAP_PREVIEW.md).

---

## What Ordella gets

Ordella gains **validated product signal**: integration friction reports, webhook delivery metrics, documentation gaps, and module stability data under real workloads. Partner and retailer cohorts supply **reference architectures** and launch quotes where agreed.

Feedback flows through [FEEDBACK_LOOP.md](./FEEDBACK_LOOP.md) into engineering prioritization; aggregate metrics feed [SUCCESS_METRICS.md](./SUCCESS_METRICS.md) and [ROLL_OUT_STRATEGY.md](./ROLL_OUT_STRATEGY.md) decisions. Participants consent to anonymized usage telemetry in sandbox and pilot tenants (placeholder—legal terms TBD).

The beta is a partnership: we ship fixes and doc updates on a published cadence ([COMMUNICATION_PLAN.md](./COMMUNICATION_PLAN.md)); participants ship integrations or pilots and report blockers promptly.

## Timeline and phases

<!-- PLACEHOLDER dates—replace with leadership-approved schedule. -->

| Phase | Name | Duration (placeholder) | Focus |
|-------|------|------------------------|--------|
| **0** | Waitlist & screening | Ongoing | Intake via [WAITLIST_FLOW.md](./WAITLIST_FLOW.md) |
| **1** | Closed alpha | 8 weeks | Core API, Event Bus, webhooks; developer + partner cohort A |
| **2** | Expanded beta | 12 weeks | Retailer pilots; partner certification; autonomy/AI preview modules |
| **3** | Hardening | 6 weeks | Production promotion, SLA draft, GA readiness |
