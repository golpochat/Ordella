## Purpose of the launch

The launch introduces **Ordella — the Retail Operating System** to the market as a credible platform—not a slide-deck concept. We communicate one coherent story: retail’s fragmentation problem, Ordella’s unified tenant/API/event model, and a path for **developers**, **partners**, and **retailers** to build and run on that foundation.

Launch is not “feature dump day.” It coordinates **ordella.com**, **docs.ordella.com**, developer and partner programs, press, and narrative assets so every channel reinforces the same proof points documented in [LAUNCH_NARRATIVE.md](../LAUNCH_NARRATIVE.md). Preview modules stay honestly labeled per [brand voice](../../brand/VOICE_AND_TONE.md) and [changelog](../../docs/public/changelog.md).

Internal teams use this timeline folder as the **single checklist** for readiness; external audiences see polished surfaces only when [CHECKLIST.md](./CHECKLIST.md) gates pass.

## Launch goals

1. **Narrative clarity** — “Retail Operating System” understood; fragmentation → unification story repeatable by sales, partners, and press.  
2. **Developer activation** — Integrators complete [10–15 minute onboarding](../../developer-onboarding/OVERVIEW.md) and first API + webhook in sandbox.  
3. **Partner pipeline** — Qualified ISV/SI applications enter [partner onboarding](../../partner-program/ONBOARDING.md); first certified listings on roadmap.  
4. **Retailer interest** — Demo requests and pilot conversations from target segments; enterprise can access architecture/compliance packs.  
5. **Beta graduation** — Beta cohort metrics meet [SUCCESS_METRICS](../../beta-program/SUCCESS_METRICS.md) gates before GA announcement.  
6. **Press & social proof** — Tier-2 tech/trade coverage and launch video views; factual alignment with [fact sheet](../../press-kit/FACT_SHEET.md).

Goals deliberately exclude vanity metrics without quality bars (raw signups without activation, unpublished partner logos without certification).
