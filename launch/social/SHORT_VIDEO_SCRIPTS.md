## Script 1 — “Five truths” (15s)

**Hook on screen (0–2s):** Five systems. Five truths.

| Time | Scene | Narration |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 0–3s | Split screen: POS, web, spreadsheet, apps | “Retail wasn’t meant to run on five different truths.” |
| 3–8s | Chaos montage → calm single dashboard | “Ordella unifies operations, commerce, and intelligence—on one platform.” |
| 8–12s | Logo resolve | “Ordella. The Retail Operating System.” |
| 12–15s | End card | “Live today. ordella.com” |

**CTA (on-screen):** ordella.com · **Button text:** See the launch

**Music:** Tension → single confident chord on logo.
