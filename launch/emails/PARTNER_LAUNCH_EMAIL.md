## Email body

Hi {{first_name}},

Today **Ordella launches publicly**—and we’re opening the **Ordella Partner Program** to technology companies that extend the Retail Operating System through apps, integrations, and services.

**Partner program overview**

Ordella partners integrate against documented APIs and the [Partner API](../../docs/public/partners/partner-api.md), certify for quality and security, and optionally list on the marketplace where retailers discover solutions that already speak a **unified tenant and event model**. Tiers—**Registered, Silver, Gold, and Platinum**—scale benefits with certification depth, support SLAs, and go-to-market participation. Full structure: [partner-program/PARTNER_TIERS.md](../../partner-program/PARTNER_TIERS.md) and [public partner program](../../docs/public/partners/partner-program.md).

**Revenue share summary**

Qualifying **marketplace subscriptions** and defined **attach** transactions earn commission per your partner agreement. Payouts run monthly in arrears; statements and exports live in the partner area of the Developer Portal. Public mechanics (not binding rates) are in [REVENUE_SHARE.md](../../partner-program/REVENUE_SHARE.md) and [docs revenue share](../../docs/public/partners/revenue-share.md). Specific percentages are provided during contracting—we do not publish rates in marketing email.

Silver certification, webhook reliability, and security review are required before listing. Onboarding typically spans **30–60 days** to marketplace-ready: [partner ONBOARDING.md](../../partner-program/ONBOARDING.md).
