## Email body

Hi {{first_name}},

Retail runs on too many truths. POS says one thing. Ecommerce says another. Inventory lives in a export from Tuesday. Your teams spend hours reconciling instead of serving customers—and shoppers feel the gap in a single visit.

**Today, Ordella launches** as **the Retail Operating System**: one multi-tenant platform for store operations, commerce, data, and intelligence. Catalog, orders, inventory, pricing, and promotions share the same foundation. When a sale completes in-store or online, every system that needs to know—knows.

**Platform benefits for your business**

- **One source of truth** across locations and channels  
- **Real-time operations** instead of overnight batch surprises  
- **Modular adoption**—start with what you need, scale without rebuilding per region  
- **Enterprise-ready documentation** for security and architecture review  

**AI + autonomy highlights**

Ordella embeds **intelligence in the workflow**, not in another disconnected dashboard. The **AI Assistant** helps operators ask plain-language questions grounded in live data. The **Retail Genome** connects customers, products, and locations for smarter segmentation and planning. The **Autonomous Retail Engine** automates repeatable work—replenishment signals, pricing adjustments, promotions—**under policies you control**, with human override and audit trails. Preview modules are clearly labeled; your governance team stays in charge.

Whether you run a focused pilot or evaluate enterprise rollout, we’ll map Ordella to your locations, channels, and integration landscape.
