## Email body

Hi {{first_name}},

I’m reaching out from **Ordella** with news scheduled for **{{embargo_datetime}}**: we’re launching **Ordella**, **the Retail Operating System**—a multi-tenant platform that unifies store operations, commerce, data, and intelligence for retailers, developers, and technology partners.

**Short pitch**

Retail fractured across POS, ecommerce, inventory, and analytics. Teams reconcile spreadsheets after close; customers feel inconsistency across channels. Ordella replaces that patchwork with **one tenant model, one API (`https://api.ordella.com/v1`), and one real-time event stream**—plus embedded AI ([AI Assistant](../../docs/public/systems/ai-assistant.md), [Retail Genome](../../docs/public/systems/retail-genome.md)) and policy-bound automation ([Autonomous Retail Engine](../../docs/public/systems/autonomous-engine.md)). Developers integrate via sandbox and webhooks; partners certify and list on the marketplace; enterprises review architecture and compliance documentation.

Launch positioning also references the **Autonomous Retail Operating System**; our standard tagline remains **The Retail Operating System** ([branding](../../docs/public/_config/branding.md)).
