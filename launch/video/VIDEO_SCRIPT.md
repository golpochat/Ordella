## 1. Opening Scene — “The Retail Problem”

**Cross-reference:** [Launch Narrative §1 — The Retail Crisis](../LAUNCH_NARRATIVE.md) · [Homepage — Problem statement](../../website/copy/homepage.md#problem-statement)

### Scene description

- **Open:** Handheld or steadicam through a busy store floor—queues, label guns, associates switching between tablet, radio, and legacy POS.  
- **Cut:** Back office—manager staring at mismatched dashboards, Excel exports, sticky notes.  
- **Insert:** Split-screen montage—ecommerce promo live while shelf tag unchanged; empty peg; customer checking phone for stock that system says exists.  
- **Visual motif:** Five app windows / vendor logos (generic, unbranded) floating over the store—lines tangled between them.  
- **Close section:** Slow push on associate’s face—decision delayed, customer waiting.

### Narration

> Retail was never meant to run on five different truths.  
> Every sale. Every shelf. Every channel. Fighting the same battle—fragmented systems, manual fixes, decisions that arrive too late.  
> Margin shrinks. Teams burn out. And the customer feels it in a single visit.

### Pacing notes

- **0:00–0:04** — Ambient store sound up (beeps, carts, murmur). Narration enters on second 2.  
- **0:04–0:10** — Faster cuts (0.5–1 sec each); narration steady, not rushed.  
- **0:10–0:14** — Hold on associate/customer beat; last line lands on silence dip.

### Emotional tone

Frustration, complexity, cognitive overload. Viewer should recognize their own stack—not feel attacked.

### Music / atmosphere

- Low, pulsing tension bed (minor key).  
- Subtle glitch textures on cut transitions (suggest system friction).  
- No heroic melody yet—keep it grounded.

## 2. The Turning Point — “There Has to Be a Better Way”

**Cross-reference:** [Launch Narrative §2 — The Opportunity](../LAUNCH_NARRATIVE.md) · [Homepage — Solution statement](../../website/copy/homepage.md#solution-statement)

### Scene description

- **Hard cut or audio drop:** Chaos stops. Single store aisle—cleaner frame, softer light.  
- **Visual:** Shoppers expect seamless pickup on phone; split to back end still batch-processing “yesterday.”  
- **Metaphor:** Tangled lines begin to straighten into one luminous path through the store.  
- **Text (optional, 1 frame):** “Expectations moved. Systems didn’t.”

### Narration

> Shoppers moved faster than the stack behind them.  
> There has to be a better way—not another app.  
> One system. One heartbeat. Real time.

### Pacing notes

- **0:14–0:16** — 1.5 sec silence or room tone only (turning point breath).  
- **0:16–0:22** — Slower shots (2–3 sec); narration intimate, forward-leaning.

### Emotional tone

Shift from frustration to quiet conviction. Curiosity replaces chaos.

### Music / atmosphere

- Tension bed fades; single piano or synth note holds.  
- Light harmonic lift on “Real time.”

## 3. Reveal — “This Is Ordella”

**Cross-reference:** [Launch Narrative §3 — What is Ordella?](../LAUNCH_NARRATIVE.md) · [Brand Overview](../../brand/BRAND_OVERVIEW.md) · [Platform copy — hero](../../website/copy/platform.md#page-hero)

### Scene description

- **Reveal:** Black or deep navy → Ordella logo resolves (full wordmark—see [Logo Guidelines](../../brand/LOGO_GUIDELINES.md)).  
- **Cinematic UI montage (stylized, not literal dump):** Unified platform hub—POS, storefront, admin, map of locations pulsing in sync.  
- **Global:** Dot map or region lights—multi-location, multi-region.  
- **Keywords on screen (staggered, optional):** REAL-TIME · AI-NATIVE · GLOBAL

### Narration

> This is Ordella.  
> The **Autonomous Retail Operating System**.  
> Real-time. AI-native. Built for retail at global scale—one tenant, one truth, from the store floor to the edge.

### Pacing notes

- **0:22** — Logo hit on beat one (music downbeat).  
- **0:24–0:28** — “Autonomous Retail Operating System” — slight pause before and after for weight.  
- **0:28–0:32** — UI montage accelerates; narration completes before product montage.

### Emotional tone

Bold, assured, inevitable—not arrogant. “Finally exists” energy.

### Music / atmosphere

- Full theme enters: modern orchestral + electronic hybrid (think precision, not carnival).  
- Wide stereo; sub hit on logo.

**Note:** Public tagline remains **“The Retail Operating System”** ([branding.md](../../docs/public/_config/branding.md)); video may use “Autonomous Retail Operating System” for launch positioning—align end cards with brand/legal if needed.

## 4. Product Highlights

> **Cloud and edge** — global scale, local resilience.  
> **Retail Genome** — the intelligence graph that connects customers, products, and places.

### Pacing notes

- **0:32–0:52** — ~3 sec per highlight; sync narration line starts on visual beat.

### Emotional tone

Controlled excitement—precision engineering, not magic tricks.

### Music / atmosphere

- Theme stays driving; light percussive ticks on each product name.  
- Brief filter sweep between beats for continuity.

## 5. The Transformation

**Cross-reference:** [Launch Narrative §7 — The Impact](../LAUNCH_NARRATIVE.md) · [Homepage — Solution / AI highlights](../../website/copy/homepage.md)

### Scene description

- **Before/after (subtle):** Same store—earlier tension vs. calm flow.  
- Associate helps customer instantly; manager reviews one dashboard; promotion rolls out synchronized on web + POS.  
- **Automation:** Replenishment cart rolls; staffing chart adjusts; exception flagged with clear “approve” path (human in loop).  
- **Human moment:** Associate smiles—technology invisible.

### Narration

> Operations automate the repeatable.  
> Decisions get intelligent—not improvised.  
> Retail becomes predictable enough to plan—and flexible enough to win.  
> Teams stop fighting the stack. They start running the business.

### Pacing notes

- **0:52–1:04** — Warmer color grade; longer shots (2–3 sec).  
- Last line holds on team shot **1 sec** before future section.

### Emotional tone

Relief, empowerment, competence. Viewer should feel “we could run like this.”

### Music / atmosphere

- Theme softens; add warm pads.  
- Reduce percussion; let narration lead.

> Autonomous commerce. A global intelligence network.  
> Simulation-driven operations. AI-powered decisioning—with guardrails you control.

### Pacing notes

- Final line: pause **0.5 sec** post “guardrails you control.”

### Emotional tone

Visionary, expansive, responsible. Hope with accountability.

### Music / atmosphere

- Theme swells (not max—save peak for logo end card).  
- Ethereal top line; keep kick drum restrained.
